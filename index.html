Platform

From code to a seat that moves.

Software and hardware are developed under one roof. As a result, every MSIMS simulator shares the same standards for controls, scoring and maintenance.

4DOF
Motion Base that follows the ground surface and waves in the simulation
60Hz
Motion telemetry from software to platform
100
Points in the scoring rubric for every training session
0
Internet connections needed during training
Real control hardware
Force-feedback steering wheels, dual joystick levers in ISO and SAE patterns, ship consoles, throttle levers and drone remote controllers.
Instructor console on a tablet
Instructors launch scenarios, change weather and wind, and monitor trainees from a tablet.
Scoring and transcripts
Trainee name and ID records, session reports and printable competency transcripts.
Offline and licensed
Runs without internet in labs, colleges or exhibition halls. Licences are locked to the device.
VR modules
VR experiences for Meta Quest 3 headsets, suited to exhibitions and public learning.
Malaysian environments
Paddy fields, oil palm and rubber estates, Langkawi waters, construction sites and village roads, modelled on how they look locally.
